Generated from sofle.keymap by scripts/gen-keymap-art.
Do not edit by hand - edit the keymap and regenerate.

Each key stacks up to three lines:
    1  tap        what the key sends
    2  shifted    what Shift gives (punctuation / symbols)
    3  hold       what holding it gives (modifier names)
A line is omitted when no key in that row uses it.

    ...   transparent - falls through to the layer below
    HELD  the key you are holding to be on this layer
    blank nothing bound

The two keys between the halves are the ENCODER PUSH-BUTTONS.

-- NOTES ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Home-row mods are GACS: GUI Alt Ctrl Shift, going inward, mirrored.
  They only resolve as a hold if the next key is on the OTHER hand, and
  only after 150ms of idle - so same-hand rolls still type letters.

  Alt+hjkl walks nvim splits and tmux panes as one space - a
  left-hand hold plus a right-hand tap, no layer needed.

  The Ctrl+b prefix (tmux and herdr) is the exception: b is a LEFT-half
  key, so the cross-hand guard refuses a left home-row Ctrl and you get
  "db". Use either bottom-row corner Ctrl.

  Hold NAV + MEDIA (or NAV + RET) for the ADJ layer.

  The bottom-row corners are Ctrl, not Shift. Home-row Ctrl is on D
  behind the cross-hand guard, so it can only fire with a right-hand
  key - every left-hand Ctrl chord (<C-d> <C-u> <C-w> <C-r> <C-v>,
  and Ctrl+b) needs a guard-free corner Ctrl. Shift moved one
  row down to the thumb keys.

  Both Shift keys together = caps word: UVM_INFO and SCREAMING_PARAMS
  type in one gesture and end themselves at the space.

  Of a TKL's right-side overflow, [ and ] are on base - [ where a TKL
  has it, after P, and ] on the bottom-right corner. Shift composes,
  so { and } (paragraph motions) are one gesture. Only \ and = stay
  layer-bound: NAV+8 is \, NAV+- is =, and , + . is also \. NAV
  keeps [ and ] as well, as a fallback.
